Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Will my photo be accepted by immigration authorities?

Yes! Our tool follows official ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) standards used by 30+ countries. We provide real-time compliance checks to ensure your photo meets all requirements. Plus, we offer a 100% money-back guarantee if your photo is rejected due to our error.

How does the AI adjustment work?

Our AI automatically removes your background and replaces it with the required color (white or light grey), adjusts lighting to meet standards, and ensures your pose is correct. You can also manually adjust zoom, position, brightness, and rotation for perfect results. It's completely optional - you have full control!

What do I get for $4.99?

You get two high-resolution digital files (300 DPI) ready for printing: (1) a single passport photo perfectly sized for your country's requirements, and (2) a 4x6 inch sheet with multiple copies for your convenience. No watermarks, no subscriptions, instant download.

Can I use a photo from my phone?

Absolutely! Just make sure the photo is clear, well-lit, and at least 600x600 pixels. Our app works perfectly on all devices - phone, tablet, or computer. Take a selfie (with good lighting) or ask someone to take your photo.

Which countries are supported?

We support 30+ countries including US, UK, Canada, Australia, all Schengen countries (Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, etc.), India, China, Japan, Brazil, Mexico, and many more. Select your country in the app to see specific requirements for passports and visas.

How long does it take?

Most users complete the entire process in under 5 minutes: Upload photo (30 sec) → AI adjustment (1 min) → Manual fine-tuning (2 min) → Payment & download (1 min). That's it!

Is my payment secure?

100% secure. All payments are processed through Stripe, the same secure payment system used by Amazon, Google, and millions of businesses worldwide. We never see or store your payment information. Your transaction is fully encrypted and secure.
